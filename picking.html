How the scoring works

Within each tier you're ranked 1 to N in three categories (1 = best). Each rank is multiplied by the category's weight, and the three results are added up — golf rules: lowest total picks first. Career sales break ties.

Campaign window

Spring + Summer event sales, Jan 1 – Aug 4, 2026 — straight from VectorConnect (booth, service events, and service calls from booth leads).

  • Weighted shift average = (Traditional CPO × 2.4 + Key CPO + 5 × team average) ÷ (shifts + 5). Unsubmitted shifts count as $0.
  • Drops: every 10 shifts worked per type removes your lowest shift of that type before averaging. Misses can never be dropped.
  • Missing data ranks last in that category — it is never treated as zero, and never ignored.
  • Tiers require both the shift-average bar and the sales bars (Elite: 12-mo ≥ $200k · Varsity: 12-mo ≥ $120k + career ≥ $150k). No exceptions.

Elite What it takes to be Elite

The numbers (checked automatically)

  • Shift average (raw, last 12 months, misses count $0, min 10 shifts total): Key ≥ $3,250 or Traditional ≥ $2,200
  • Career sales ≥ $500,000 (or $300,000 in the previous year)
  • Last 12-month sales ≥ $200,000
  • 3 campaigns on the team

The standards (confirmed at the meeting)

  • Own complete booth display (black CUTCO canopy, full event display kit, backdrop, biz samples)
  • Attended SC1 or SC2 the prior summer + PUSH countdown
  • Yearly Business Plan submitted to DM and Coordinator by the January meeting
  • 7 service event days per year, or 1 Team Service Event Blitz Week (min 5 days)
  • Attended 3/3 meetings in person, no early departures
  • Marketing: monthly CRM email + market 2 of 4 ways before every Elite event (30-mile radius)
Varsity What it takes to be Varsity

The numbers (checked automatically)

  • Shift average (raw, last 12 months, misses count $0, min 10 shifts total): Key ≥ $2,500 or Traditional ≥ $1,200
  • Career sales ≥ $150,000
  • Last 12-month sales ≥ $120,000
  • 2 campaigns on the team

The standards (confirmed at the meeting)

  • Own booth (Ultimate, Signature, Homemaker, Santoku, accessory + flatware packages, tables, black cloths, risers, canopy)
  • Attended SC1 or SC2 + PUSH countdown
  • 6 service event days per year, or 1 Team Service Event Blitz Week (min 4 days)
  • New customer, think-about-it, and past customer approaches 100% down (verified by an Elite member)
  • Attended 2/3 meetings in person, no early departures
  • Marketing: monthly CRM email + market to 3+ events per year (20-mile radius)
JV What it takes to be JV

The numbers (checked automatically)

  • Career sales ≥ $50,000
  • Previous campaign: ≥ $5,000 at the booth or ≥ $10,000 total
  • 1 campaign on the team

The standards (confirmed at the meeting)

  • Has a CRM
  • Owns the Booth in the Box program (Classic Homemaker +8, flatware chest, 2 blue cloths, can opener, pizza cutter, ice cream scoop, banner)
  • Owns 2× 5ft tables with PVC + white canopy tent
  • New customer and think-about-it approaches 90% down (verified by an Elite member)
  • Attended monthly meetings + 1/3 core event meetings in person
