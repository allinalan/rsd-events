How your score is calculated

Six categories, each turned into a score from 0 to 100, each multiplied by its weight, then added up to one total out of 100.

Weighted shift average · 45%

Your sales per shift across Key and Traditional events in the last 12 months.

Last year's sales · 15%

Your total sales last calendar year, every channel: events, realtor orders, referrals.

Event sales · 10%

What VectorConnect credits you at events only: the work the booth is staffed for.

Orders per shift · 10%

How many orders you write per shift, weighted the same way as the shift average.

Presence · 10%

Your energy behind the booth, on the slow shifts as much as the busy ones.

Teamwork · 10%

Whether you make the booth better for the people working next to you.

1. The weighted shift average

Traditional events have smaller crowds than Key events, so every Traditional dollar and order counts 1.5×. The bonus applies once you have 6 or more Traditional shifts in the window. With fewer, your Traditional shifts are left out entirely and you are scored on Key shifts alone, so one hot Traditional shift cannot carry an average.

6+ Traditional shifts: (Key $ + Traditional $ × 1.5) ÷ (Key shifts + Traditional shifts)
Fewer than 6: Key $ ÷ Key shifts

Same shift list as your My Stat Audit tab: a shift with no stat turned in counts as $0. Maricopa shifts are Key shifts.

2. Every category becomes a score from 0 to 100

For the four sales categories, whoever is lowest on the team gets 0, whoever is highest gets 100, and everyone else lands between them on a straight line. Presence and Teamwork turn 1 to 5 into 0 to 100.

Score = (your number − team's lowest) ÷ (team's highest − team's lowest) × 100

3. Scores are weighted and added

Total = Shift × 45% + Last year × 15% + Event sales × 10% + Orders × 10% + Presence × 10% + Teamwork × 10%

If a category has no data for someone, it is left out for the whole team and the rest are rescaled, so nobody is compared on a different set of numbers. The top 6 totals are the Starting 6; a tie goes to the higher weighted shift average.

What a 0 means

A 0 in a category does not mean you are failing. It means you are the lowest on this team in that one category, and someone has to be. A 100 means highest on the team, not perfect. The scale only compares the people on this page to each other.
